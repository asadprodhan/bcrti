Weekly Modules

Six-Week Learning Journey

Each week includes an overview and five topics, with Learn, Explore, Apply and Check activities.

Week 1

Study Design and the Wet-Lab Journey

Turn a research question into a sampling plan and follow the sample through preservation, extraction, library preparation and sequencing.

  1. What question should my microbiome study answer?
  2. How should I design sampling and replication?
  3. How do I collect and preserve samples?
  4. How does DNA extraction affect my results?
  5. How does DNA become sequencing data?
Week 2

From Sequencing Data to a Reproducible Bioinformatics Workflow

Inspect sequencing files and sample information, understand the role of reference databases, and record analysis decisions.

  1. What does the bioinformatics workflow do?
  2. What are FASTQ, metadata and report files?
  3. What is a reference database, and what can it tell me?
  4. How does Kraken2 classify reads?
  5. How do I make an analysis reproducible?
Week 3

Read Quality Control and Taxonomic Classification

Interpret FastQC reports, justify any read trimming, and explain the counts and assignments in a Kraken2 report.

  1. What do a read and its quality score mean?
  2. How do I interpret a FastQC report?
  3. When should I trim or filter reads?
  4. How do I run and compare read QC?
  5. What does a Kraken2 report tell me?
Week 4

Analysing Kraken2 Taxonomic Outputs

Prepare an abundance table, plot community composition and explore diversity and differences between groups.

  1. How do I prepare a valid abundance table?
  2. How do I describe community composition?
  3. What is alpha diversity, and how should I compare it?
  4. What is beta diversity, and what does an ordination show?
  5. How do I test differential abundance responsibly?
Week 5

Antimicrobial Resistance, Visualisation and Interpretation

Interpret resistance gene matches, distinguish DNA evidence from measured resistance, and present findings in clear figures and results statements.

  1. What does an AMR analysis detect?
  2. How do I screen reads or assemblies for AMR genes?
  3. How do I judge an AMR gene match?
  4. How should I visualise taxonomic and AMR results?
  5. How do I interpret and report microbiome findings?
Week 6

Course Synthesis, Knowledge Check and Completion

Connect the full workflow, reason through study design and analysis problems, and complete the cumulative assessment.

  1. Can I explain the complete sample-to-result journey?
  2. Can I diagnose a study design and QC problem?
  3. Which analysis answers my biological question?
  4. How do I turn analysis into a defensible result?
  5. Complete the cumulative knowledge check and course requirements
