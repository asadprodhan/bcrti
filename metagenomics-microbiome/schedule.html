Schedule

Six weekly live online classes via Microsoft Teams, two hours per session. Weekly activities are available in BILS.

Cohort dates will be shared with prospective learners who submit an expression of interest.

Assessment & Completion

Complete the assigned weekly activities and the 25-question cumulative assessment in Week 6. BCRTI issues a Certificate of Completion once course requirements are met.

Week 1 · Study Design and the Wet-Lab Journey

Week 12 hoursTurn a research question into a sampling plan and follow the sample through preservation, extraction, library preparation and sequencing.

Week 2 · From Sequencing Data to a Reproducible Bioinformatics Workflow

Week 22 hoursInspect sequencing files and sample information, understand the role of reference databases, and record analysis decisions.

Week 3 · Read Quality Control and Taxonomic Classification

Week 32 hoursInterpret FastQC reports, justify any read trimming, and explain the counts and assignments in a Kraken2 report.

Week 4 · Analysing Kraken2 Taxonomic Outputs

Week 42 hoursPrepare an abundance table, plot community composition and explore diversity and differences between groups.

Week 5 · Antimicrobial Resistance, Visualisation and Interpretation

Week 52 hoursInterpret resistance gene matches, distinguish DNA evidence from measured resistance, and present findings in clear figures and results statements.

Week 6 · Course Synthesis, Knowledge Check and Completion

Week 62 hoursConnect the full workflow, reason through study design and analysis problems, and complete the cumulative assessment.
