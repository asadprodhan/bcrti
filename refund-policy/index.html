BCRTI POLICIES

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

The Bioinformatics Capability and Research Training Institute (BCRTI) is committed to providing high-quality bioinformatics training and fair, transparent enrolment conditions.

1. Scope

This policy applies to individual learners purchasing BCRTI self-paced micro-credentials directly from BCRTI. Instructor-led training, group purchases and institutional arrangements may have separately agreed cancellation and refund terms, subject to applicable law.

2. Cancellation before course activation

Learners who have paid for a course but whose access to the BCRTI Interactive Learning Space (BILS) has not yet been activated may request a full refund before activation.

3. Seven-day change-of-mind refund policy

Once course access has been activated, BCRTI offers a full voluntary change-of-mind refund when both of the following conditions are satisfied:

  • The refund request is received within seven (7) calendar days (168 hours) of the recorded BILS access activation time.
  • The learner has not accessed any learning content beyond Week 1.

Both conditions must be satisfied. Once either limit is exceeded, a voluntary change-of-mind refund is no longer available.

These restrictions apply only to voluntary change-of-mind refunds and do not affect statutory consumer rights.

4. Learning activity and refund eligibility

BCRTI may review recorded course activation dates, learning activity and progress information in BILS when assessing eligibility for a voluntary change-of-mind refund.

Accessing Week 2 or later learning materials, exercises or assessments ends eligibility for a voluntary change-of-mind refund. Viewing general course information, navigation menus or the course overview alone does not count as accessing Week 2 learning content.

5. Course cancellation and technical difficulties

If BCRTI cancels a course before providing access, learners will receive a full refund.

Learners experiencing technical difficulties should contact BCRTI so that assistance can be provided. Where BCRTI cannot deliver the purchased training or applicable consumer guarantees are not met, learners may be entitled to a remedy, including a refund where required by law.

6. Exceptional circumstances

BCRTI may consider refund requests outside the voluntary change-of-mind conditions in exceptional circumstances. Such requests will be assessed individually. This does not limit any statutory consumer rights.

7. How to request a refund

To request a refund, please email asad.prodhan@bcrti.org with your name, course title, payment date and reason for requesting a refund.

BCRTI will review the request and communicate the outcome by email. Learners are not required to provide credit card details.

8. Refund processing and course access

Approved refunds will normally be processed through the original payment provider, including Stripe where applicable, and returned to the original payment method. Refund processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and financial institution.

Where a learner receives a refund and cancels their enrolment, BCRTI may terminate the associated course access, subject to applicable legal obligations.

9. Australian Consumer Law

Our services come with consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded under Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer rights or remedies available under applicable law.
