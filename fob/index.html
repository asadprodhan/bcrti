Overview

Build confidence in sequencing data analysis

Modern biological research increasingly relies on DNA and RNA sequencing. Yet many researchers encounter a major challenge after sequencing: understanding what their data represent and how to transform raw files into meaningful biological results.

This micro-credential provides a structured pathway into bioinformatics, beginning with sequencing concepts, data organisation and quality assessment, and progressing into computational environments, reproducible workflows and analysis planning. Participants develop the essential data literacy required to navigate sequencing projects with confidence and communicate effectively with collaborators and bioinformaticians.

Who this is for

Designed for researchers entering bioinformatics

This course is suitable for

  • Higher Degree by Research candidates
  • Honours and Masters students
  • Early career researchers
  • Laboratory scientists and research staff
  • Researchers beginning sequencing data analysis for the first time

Relevant research areas

  • Genomics and molecular biology
  • Microbiome and metagenomics
  • Transcriptomics and RNA-seq
  • Agriculture, environmental and biomedical sciences

Prerequisites

No prior bioinformatics or coding experience is required. Participants are expected to have a basic understanding of biological research and an interest in analysing sequencing datasets.

Learning Outcomes

What participants will be able to do

01
Understand sequencing technologies
  • Differentiate Illumina, Nanopore and PacBio platforms
  • Understand FASTQ files and metadata
  • Interpret sequencing workflows
02
Work confidently with computational environments
  • Use Linux fundamentals
  • Understand HPC concepts
  • Manage Conda environments and containers
03
Understand sequencing quality control
  • Interpret FastQC outputs
  • Understand trimming and filtering
  • Recognise common data quality issues
04
Plan reproducible bioinformatics analyses
  • Understand workflow logic
  • Recognise analysis pathways for different datasets
  • Communicate effectively with bioinformaticians and collaborators

Teaching approach

The micro-credential uses scaffolded, research-integrated learning with authentic sequencing datasets and reproducible workflows to develop computational confidence and sequencing data literacy.

Final Capstone Project

Apply bioinformatics concepts to real sequencing data

Participants complete a mini capstone project using their own research dataset or an example dataset to consolidate learning and strengthen research capability.

  • Data organisation and metadata management
  • Quality assessment and interpretation
  • Selection of appropriate analysis strategies
  • Workflow planning and reproducibility
  • Research communication and collaboration
  • Roadmap for subsequent domain-specific analyses

Academic Lead

Who you will learn from

Dr Asad Prodhan

Founder & Academic Lead, BCRTI
Adjunct Senior Lecturer in Bioinformatics,
The University of Western Australia

Dr Asad Prodhan is a molecular biologist, bioinformatician and educator specialising in genomics, practical bioinformatics and reproducible computational workflows.

His own transition from molecular biology into bioinformatics shaped his approach to teaching: complex computational skills become achievable when they are broken into practical, purposeful and reproducible steps. Through BCRTI, he applies this philosophy to help researchers move from learning bioinformatics to confidently analysing their own data independently.
